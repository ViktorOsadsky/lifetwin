Step counting

Raise sensitivity if small steps are missed; lower it if you get extra steps. Keep “ignore steps while sitting” on: without it a bouncing leg while seated counts as walking (needs your sitting position saved). Use “count twice” only if the band consistently reports about half your real steps.
