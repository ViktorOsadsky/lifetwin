# Compare mobile craft against inspected primary work

Use for a high-quality mobile/pet/game brief, especially when refining a still-weak output. A reference's reputation selects a candidate; its actual frame and useful interaction determine what transfers. These examples were inspected 23 October 2026. Historical frames are identified, not presented as current app versions.

| Primary reference | Study this relationship | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Finch developer gallery](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/finch-self-care-pet/id1528595748) | Quiet scenery, neutral task surfaces, colored selection and clear next action; pose/contact carries emotion. | Crop embedded app UI away from promotional text. A habit list is not every pet game's architecture. |
| [Rooms, Apple's 2024 screen](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/images/2024/06/apple-announces-winners-of-the-2024-apple-design-awards/article/Apple-WWDC24-Design-Awards-Rooms-240606_inline.jpg.large_2x.jpg) | Selected object remains vivid and visibly related to its named editing surface. Rich art and quiet chrome coexist. | Historical editor screenshot; not proof of the current app or a requirement for voxel 3D. |
| [Crouton, Apple's 2024 screen](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/images/2024/06/apple-announces-winners-of-the-2024-apple-design-awards/article/Apple-WWDC24-Design-Awards-Crouton-240606_inline.jpg.large_2x.jpg) | The item being chosen fills useful card area; titles, metadata and navigation have differentiated weight. | Transfer object scale/hierarchy, not recipe-browser architecture or photography into an illustrated pet. |
| [Toca Boca World developer gallery](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/toca-boca-world-game-play/id1208138685) | Dark character edges coexist with quieter architecture, material marks, overlap and specific props. | Promotional landscape stills demonstrate scene craft; captions/surrounds are not gameplay chrome. Outlines are not inherently low quality. |
| [Pok Pok official playroom](https://playpokpok.com/) and [press kit](https://playpokpok.com/press/press-kit/) | Specific authored shapes and restrained structural space can make simple art feel inhabited. | Compare actual game imagery. Yellow store surrounds or physical toys are not the app palette; tablet art does not prove portrait touch layout. |
| [Rytmos, Apple's 2024 screen](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/images/2024/06/apple-announces-winners-of-the-2024-apple-design-awards/article/Apple-WWDC24-Design-Awards-Rytmos-240606_inline.jpg.large_2x.jpg) | One dominant graphic event and intentional strong color can establish decisive hierarchy. | Yellow can work when it has a role; do not copy the hue or make all chrome equally loud. |
| [NotBoring's original game-feel breakdown](https://notbor.ing/words/the-most-satisfying-checkbox) | One routine action has immediate acknowledgment, buildup, release and early-cancel reversal. Design the relationship between phases. | Published process, not our runtime observation. Do not copy its long-press gesture, 3D or sound/haptics into every care UI. |

Additional 2026 primary studies: [Is This Seat Taken?](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=z12xq8fa) connects task cues, character expression and one confirmation surface; [Sago Mini Jinja’s Garden](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=3t6o5jec) connects specific world objects to a substantial selected-item tray; [grug](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=ux44ymcr) gives text/icons a coherent authored language on sparse white/dark/mint surfaces. Their inspected source frames are respectively landscape game, controller-enabled landscape game and promotional phone composite. Transfer the suitable relationship, not paper/yellow treatment, preschool interaction rules or a custom font engine.

For one exact frame, identify the strongest subject, quietest supporting area, useful next action and any selection/result relationship. Compare our equivalent at the same phone width, excluding device/store presentation. Inspect home and an editing/choice state; a good hero alone can hide weak controls.

Name the largest visible gap: hierarchy, authored material, object scale, type optics, framing, contact or preview visibility. Identify the production change it needs. If the scene is a schematic backdrop or the controls overwhelm the pet, change the composition/material/control family before adding effects. Apply only the relationship suited to the brief; imitation of a premium app's unrelated layout is not improvement.

Review the corrected stills first, then actual press, response, interrupted/reversed and reduced-motion behavior. A static source image cannot prove easing, haptics or responsiveness. Keep app pixels, marketing art, historical screenshots, published process and our own runtime evidence separate. Do not invent a 10/10 score or infer professional equivalence from green checks, reference count or confident language. If a reviewer still finds a material gap, retain it as unresolved and continue the relevant production pass.
