## Event / source conflicts

- Devpost cash-style labels include hardware and credits; use the description for award type and confirm actual cash with organizer.
- CREAO cash amounts differ between sources. Its full brief may resolve them.
- [Fetch.ai's own event page](https://www.fetch.ai/events/calhacks-13-0) lists $1,250/$750/$500 plus two $250 recognition awards, conflicting with Hive/Devpost $1,500/$1,000/$500. Confirm the allocation rather than choosing the larger version.
- Apify five placements total $4,600 although Hive card says $5,000. Best Actor is separately $400; a combined $5k is a plausible interpretation, not confirmed allocation.
- Hive Figma categories TBD versus five detailed Devpost entries.
- Hive First Overall specifies a MacBook while Devpost describes MacBook or Framework.
- Devpost says team required 2–4 and also lists Solo. Do not resolve this by assumption.
- Devpost gives Oct25 8 AM venue / 9 AM submission as “PST”; local timezone is daylight-saving at that date. Confirm event-local cutoff. Use an earlier internal freeze.
- Hive resources page returned unavailable during research. Sponsor full briefs and test keys may be supplied at the venue later.

General judging evaluates feasible application, intentional user experience, working functionality, creativity and technical command. The published slot is 3 minutes plus 2 minutes questions; Cartesia and Bright Data request a 2-minute sponsor story. No checked page established that all prizes can be stacked.
