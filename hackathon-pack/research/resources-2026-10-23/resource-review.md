## Why no new sponsor-named skills

The ten-skill pack already separates idea research, prize rules, backend, evaluation, demo, creative UI, broad integrations, and the three deeper Apify/Visa/Hyphenate workflows. Remaining resources add conditional requirements or implementation branches; duplicating each provider as a new skill would increase discovery cost without supplying a distinct reusable workflow. No resource established a necessary eleventh skill. The broad sponsor builder is the home for narrowly selected voice/retrieval/Forge/CREAO/provider routes; its references are loaded only when that branch is selected.

- Factory is authentication-gated (401); do not try another representation to evade it. Ask for the pack only if Factory is actually selected and its exact unseen rules matter. Fetch failed with a client exception rather than an authentication gate. Their current full briefs remain unreviewed.
- Provider docs prove documented capabilities, not provisioned credentials or runtime success. Bitdeer, CRS and Cartesia onboarding remains an implementation gate for operations needing access. No developer token, key, real identity fixture or query containing a credential is archived.
- Context.dev People was linked but its overview could not be fetched by the text tool. People enrichment was not necessary for this pack scope, so no new personal-data workflow was fabricated.
- Visa/Hyphenate/Apify content reconciliation is owned by the dedicated skill authors. This ledger maps their roles and known material conflicts; their dedicated references hold the detailed evidence.
- CREAO callback security was exercised with a local simulator only; see [the local exercise](local-exercise.md). This is executable verification of a narrow integration pattern, not a live CREAO success claim.
