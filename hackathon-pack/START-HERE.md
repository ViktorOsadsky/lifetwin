# Your Cal Hacks skill pack

**My best current bet is RecallDesk:** an evidence-driven exception desk for small resale / consignment shops using separate sales and consignor systems. It carries a recalled or refunded unit through quarantine, ownership, commission correction, remedy evidence and settlement reconciliation. The opportunity is the handoff between systems; ordinary recall scanning and consignor accounting already exist.

Start with [the complete idea audit](research/ideas/idea-audit.md), [all prizes and ranked tracks](skills/hackathon-prize-strategy/references/event-snapshot.md), and [the work ledger](PROJECT_STATUS.md). Research is dated October 23, 2026. Prize details can change during the event.

## The recommendation and its evidence

The strongest concrete demand artifact is [Consignor Connect's Rose refund guide](https://help.consignorconnect.com/knowledge/how-to-refund-in-rose): a refund in Square still requires manual changes to the Rose sales batch and consignor payout activity; an already-paid consignor requires a separate charge adjustment. [Bushel & Peck's actual store agreement](https://shopbushelandpeck.com/pages/consignor-guide-agreement) connects recall-related removal, ownership and refund/commission policy. [Another independent store](https://www.theelephantstrunk4kids.com/our-standards) explicitly performs recall screening. These document real work and a manual workaround. They do **not** establish a paid pilot, the frequency of recall-specific exceptions or willingness to pay for this proposed product.

The novelty claim is deliberately narrow: a recall-evidence-driven handoff into the existing finance workflow, with correct stock disposition and money states. SimpleConsign and Ricochet already cover substantial accounting/return functions; recall apps already detect products and maintain evidence. Do not pitch “first AI recall scanner” or “first consignment ledger.” The audit includes the strongest negative discoveries and what would kill this idea.

**The demo:** return a suspect item. A routine refund is insufficient: its stock stays quarantined pending exact-model/serial evidence; proposed consignor adjustments follow the store's documented policy; an in-kind replacement remains separate from cash. Retry a settlement event and the cash total stays unchanged. Ask a judge to change the serial requirement or remedy. Every result links to its source and uncertainty.

**Weekend slice:** one inventory CSV, one synthetic store policy, two real official recall notices and one reviewable closeout packet. Use public notices as real inputs; label stock, payouts, bank events and actions synthetic until an authorized integration exists. No product-safety certification, automatic chargeback or manufacturer claim submission. Public sources do not establish a reseller's remedy eligibility. The supplied fixture is policy feasibility, not a complete agent or retailer integration.

## Best ideas after disconfirmation

1. **RecallDesk:** best combined finance story, public trigger and adversarial demo. The cross-system handoff has documented manual work; recall-specific budget and superiority to incumbents need a pilot. Kill it if the result is only scanning plus a standard ledger.
2. **Grant Runway:** help a small nonprofit plan already-approved commitments when grant reimbursements arrive late. Strong published evidence of cash disruption; restrict the claim to feasible cash timing, because Ramp/Sage already cover grant spending and accounting. Best if you have domain access.
3. **ShelfLife:** check an actual refurbished Chromebook listing against a student's required support horizon and application features. Easiest complete shopping demo, but AUE-aware comparisons exist; exact listing uncertainty is the wedge. Consumer willingness to pay is unverified.

Repair shopping, furniture fit, generic financial close, construction pay applications and pallet analysis were also researched and downgraded because direct products already cover the proposed core. See the audit for primary links, eight ranked concepts, data access, stress cases, measured fixtures and unrun kill tests. No idea is certified globally unique.

## Installation

The pack's installer preflights all names, refuses existing different skills and never overwrites them. It defaults to the user's Codex skills directory; an explicit destination can be supplied for another compatible agent.

```sh
python3 scripts/install_skills.py
python3 scripts/install_skills.py --apply
```

The first command previews, the second copies new skill folders. If a destination already contains a different skill, compare it manually and choose a new destination; do not delete existing work just to install the pack. The final response reports whether installation was actually performed in this session.

## Honest access limits

Its skill distinguishes that event scope from future schema-based onboarding. Visa’s starter pack says proprietary APIs will not be provided and external APIs are optional; its skill supports any shopping stage and keeps Visa payment/MCP recipes optional. Local policy checks are not a payment integration. Authenticated Apify runs and cloud Actor builds were not performed. Skills teach these paths and record current sources, but full live operational verification needs the actual sandbox and authorization.

The deliverables are tested, reviewed and iterated within those boundaries. “Perfect,” guaranteed prize success, proof that no competitor exists, exact-product demand and live sponsor mastery are not claims the evidence supports.
