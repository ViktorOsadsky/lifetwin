# Reference collection and analysis

This is the research behind the painted-card direction. It holds 35 verified captures from shipped apps, games and award winners, each reduced to the one lesson we took from it. The images themselves are on the private Claude Design canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/Smu6yoUWwhtuJCvdJsqVne, page "1 · References"). They are not committed, because they are third-party screenshots.

Sources were captured from the App Store, official sites and press pages on 2026-10-24.

The lead reference is the owner's set of Cal Hacks sponsor bear cards. It gave us the painted bear, the warm cocoa outline, the cream muzzle and belly, one costume per card, and the bronze, silver and holo tiers.

## How the analysis shaped the design

1. **The bear owns the stage** (Pokémon Sleep, Pou, My Talking Tom 2): the twin is about 55–60% of the window, the controls stay in fixed places, and there is one primary action per screen.
2. **The pose is the data** (Gentler Streak, Finch, Tamagotchi): state shows on the twin first, and numbers sit one tap away.
3. **One room, one verb** (Pou, Nori study): the dock is always Customize · Talk · Fortune.
4. **Building the bear** (Rilakkuma, Kumamon, Care Bears, Duolingo): one head template, a bean body, limbs growing from the outline, and age shown on the muzzle and accessories rather than as frailty.
5. **Customize orbits the bear** (Talking Angela 2, Memoji, Duolingo Avatar, CapWords, Bears Gratitude): a big live preview, icon tabs with words, and die-cut sticker items.
6. **The fortune is a ceremony for an honest result** (Pokémon Sleep count-up, Moonlitt, Golden Thread, Co–Star, The Pattern, WeCroak, with Nebula as the warning): a dusk light change and one lit orb, then a plain-words result, never a gauge with a number.
7. **One control grammar** (Duolingo, Headspace, Tide Guide, Pok Pok): lip buttons, picture tiles and mascot-led privacy.

Later rounds added the owner's feedback: softer borders, calmer backgrounds, a normal room seen from slightly above, one consistent bear across 13 painted poses, and a personal results page built from real model output. See `painted-card-direction.md`.
