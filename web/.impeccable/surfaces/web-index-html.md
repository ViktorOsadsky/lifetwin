## Direction contract

THESIS: LifeTwin is a pet that keeps four things apart: Looks, Game health, Your answers, Experiments. The page refuses the health-app hero (big number, phone mockup, feature cards).
OWN-WORLD: The pinned habitat: seamless sky fading into the mist page ground, meadow, upper-left sun, twin and Pip with contact shadows. Four round glass jars sitting on the meadow, each holding one kind of thing, labelled in Bricolage. Blue action, green only for growth, calm ink text.
STORY: Meet the twin and Pip; see the four jars; scroll and open each jar to see what goes in and what it can never change; try the demo.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed habitat filling ~80vh. Twin centre-left and Pip beside it at large scale; four jars arranged around them on the meadow, two each side. Headline "A pet that keeps your health honest." top-left in Bricolage ExtraBold; one line under it; single filled "Try the demo" pill top-right and repeated under the headline.
FORM: Surface structure 7 of 7 on my ordered list (four separate jars), seed key 747e8954. Signature interaction: hovering or focusing a jar lifts it and its lid, and the twin reacts (glance, mood) only to the jars that may affect it.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
