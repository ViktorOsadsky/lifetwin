# Packaged model card

**Version:** `personal-cox-v2-20261024T182404Z` (see [assets/model.json](assets/model.json) for the machine-readable version).

**Task:** all-cause cumulative survival at years 1–5 after a current profile assessment, ages 35–79. Eleven inputs are used. A penalized additive Cox model includes separate low/high BMI and short/long sleep terms. The packaged JSON/NumPy artifact reproduces the original lifelines model at those five horizons without loading a pickle.

**Development:** NHANES 2007–2012 fitting participants (`n=9,109`), validation participants (`n=2,570`); later 2013–2014 cycle test (`n=3,826`, 260 deaths during all available follow-up, 204 by year five). On the later cycle, C-index was **0.790** versus **0.727** for an age/sex model. Five-year censoring-adjusted Brier score was **0.04691** versus **0.04823**. Mean predicted versus observed five-year survival was **94.27% versus 94.67%** overall and **87.42% versus 88.82%** for ages 65–79. This is temporal validation within NHANES, not external-population validation. The 35–49 group had only 25 deaths by year five.

**Historical neural benchmark (not the displayed Cox score):** retrained on the identical split, DeepSurv had C-index **0.7974** and five-year Brier **0.04647**. The paired 95% bootstrap interval for its C-index advantage over Cox was **−0.0006 to +0.0156**. It had worse ages 65–79 calibration and a strong inverse older-profile BMI response. Cox remains selected for the app's displayed score and supported percentages. In held PR #126, displayed score/reveal outputs also use Cox, while the shared lab still loads/computes a neural result that the app does not read. This is a product-output distinction, not a claim that neural execution or artifacts were removed.

**Data and interpretation:** The training file uses [2019 public-use linked mortality](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/linked-data/mortality-files/index.html). For some public-use records, CDC substitutes synthetic follow-up times; vital status is unchanged. Age 80+ is [top-coded to 80](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2007/DataFiles/DEMO_E.htm). The model is not survey-weighted to a US national target population and has not been externally validated. BMI above 27 has a near-zero, slightly inverse fitted association; it is **not** evidence that gaining weight helps. As [TRIPOD+AI](https://www.bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378) notes, discrimination and calibration are separate aspects of prediction performance.

**Current app interpretation:** Main displays the negated Cox `log_relative_hazard_score`, so higher is better, plus “Healthier than X of 100 people your age,” a same-sex, nearby-age survey-reference percentile. It is not a nationally representative ranking. Main's one-time reveal still uses the CDC life-table wrapper. [Current implementation](../../lib/README-model.md).

**Conditional proposal, not merged:** [PR #126](https://github.com/ViktorOsadsky/lifetwin/pull/126), reviewed at `02f5716`, remains open under an owner hold. It would replace the score with `exp(median band s − s(profile))`, excluding Cox age/sex terms from both; **1.0 is the same-sex, nearby-age survey group's median and higher is better**. There is no separate lower-is-better hazard-index getter. The latest proposal uses yearly one-year Cox steps at ages 35–79, assuming other inputs stay fixed. Its 50%/75%/25% survival crossings supply `expectedAge`/`p25Age`/`p75Age`. Unreached thresholds at 110 would use sentinel 110 with `openEnded`/`p25OpenEnded`/`p75OpenEnded` and a **110+** headline, not an observed or validated crossing. Ages 18–34 and 80–85 would retain the extrapolated CDC fallback; the method-boundary jumps remain unresolved. [Contract, flags and current/proposed status](../../../docs/handoffs/hazard-projection-docs.md).

**Release scope:** This is a labeled hackathon research model, not a clinical prediction tool. Complete, supported profiles only; measured survival horizons are years 1–5 for assessment ages 35–79. Neither the proposed repeated one-year projection nor its fallback is validated personal life expectancy; nothing past 79 is validated in the rough guide. The five-year metrics above do not validate its lifetime tail, open-ended display or discontinuities between methods. No exact death date, diagnosis, guaranteed survival or causal change in survival is supported. [Root projection definition](../../../model_handoff/lifetwin_on_device/README.md#projection-definitions).

**Checks and guidance:** [test/golden_vectors.json](test/golden_vectors.json) checks the frozen Cox arithmetic, not the long-range projection's calibration. Guideline cards in [lib/src/assessment.dart](lib/src/assessment.dart) are a separate educational system; neither quiz/game health nor cosmetic settings change medical inputs or predictions.
