# Packaged Cox model card

**Version:** `personal-cox-bmi35-v3-20261024` in `assets/model.json`.

**Task:** all-cause cumulative survival at years 1–5 for complete, supported profiles with assessment ages 35–79. Eleven inputs are used. The frozen JSON and support reference reproduce the evaluated lifelines Cox model in Dart, without Python on the device. The dense neural hazard model is separate and unchanged.

**Training and evaluation:** 9,109 NHANES 2007–2012 participants for fitting, 2,570 for validation, and 3,826 from 2013–2014 for a later-cycle test. The later-cycle C-index is **0.7914**, five-year IPCW Brier is **0.04686**, and mean predicted versus observed five-year survival is **94.28% versus 94.67%**. For ages 65–79 it is **87.48% versus 88.82%**. See the [full evaluation](../../../web/survival_lab/evaluation.json) and [refit report](../../../web/survival_lab/MODEL_CARD.md). Differences from Cox v2 are small and do not prove an overall accuracy gain. This is later-cycle evaluation within NHANES, not external-cohort validation.

**Historical neural benchmark (not the displayed Cox score):** On the earlier Cox v2 comparison split, DeepSurv had C-index **0.7974** and five-year Brier **0.04647**. The paired 95% bootstrap interval for its C-index advantage over Cox v2 was **−0.0006 to +0.0156**. It had worse ages 65–79 calibration and a strong inverse older-profile BMI response. These numbers do not compare DeepSurv with this v3 Cox refit. Cox remains selected for the app's displayed score and supported percentages. The shared lab loads/computes a neural result that the app does not read.

**BMI encoding:** BMI below 23 uses `max(23 − BMI, 0)/5`. BMI from 23 through 35 is flat by feature design. Above 35, `max(BMI − 35, 0)/5` has a fitted coefficient **+0.0348066**. Cox v2 instead had a slightly negative coefficient above 27 and produced the inverse high-BMI direction. The refit has 1,566 fitting participants at BMI ≥35 and no fitting BMI above 84.87. Neither feature direction is a causal estimate of weight change.

**Data and interpretation:** The training file uses [2019 public-use linked mortality](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/linked-data/mortality-files/index.html). For some public-use records, CDC substitutes synthetic follow-up times; vital status is unchanged. Age 80+ is [top-coded to 80](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2007/DataFiles/DEMO_E.htm). The model is not survey-weighted to a US national target population and has not been externally validated. As [TRIPOD+AI](https://www.bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378) notes, discrimination and calibration are separate aspects of prediction performance.

**Current app interpretation:** The app displays `median band s − s(profile)`, where `s` excludes Cox age/sex terms. **0 is the same-sex, nearby-age survey group's middle; positive is healthier and higher is better.** This centred log-ratio is not a probability or nationally representative ranking. The earlier negative-log score and 1.0-relative index are superseded display scales; history uses `cox_centered_bmi35_v2` and excludes other scales. No separate lower-is-better hazard-index getter exists. [Current implementation](../../lib/README-model.md).

**One-time rough reveal:** The app blends CDC ages with yearly one-year Cox projection summaries. Projection weight is zero through 35, ramps to 1 at 45, stays 1 through 70, fades to zero at 79 and remains zero thereafter. Projection 50%/75%/25% crossings contribute to `expectedAge`/`p25Age`/`p75Age`; CDC contributes its mean/percentile ages. A missing projection threshold uses sentinel 110, retaining its open-ended flag only if the blended value remains at least 110. Retained flags show **110+**, not validated crossings. Rough guide applies where CDC has majority weight (completed ages 18–39 and 75–85). The direct lifespan helper floors a fractional model age for projection, retaining the fractional weight; confirmed app profiles still require whole-year age. Seam trends observed under Cox v2 need to be rechecked with this refit. [Contract and limits](../../../docs/handoffs/hazard-projection-docs.md).

**Release scope:** This is a labeled hackathon research model, not a clinical prediction tool. Complete, supported profiles only; measured survival horizons are years 1–5 for assessment ages 35–79. Neither the repeated one-year projection nor its CDC blend is validated personal life expectancy; nothing past 79 is validated in the rough guide. The five-year metrics above do not validate its lifetime tail, open-ended display, blending or aging trends. No exact death date, diagnosis, guaranteed survival or causal change in survival is supported. [Root projection definition](../../../model_handoff/lifetwin_on_device/README.md#projection-definitions).

**Checks and guidance:** [test/golden_vectors.json](test/golden_vectors.json) checks the frozen Cox arithmetic, not the long-range projection's calibration. Guideline cards in [lib/src/assessment.dart](lib/src/assessment.dart) are a separate educational system; neither quiz/game health nor cosmetic settings change medical inputs or predictions.
